NOTES

Catch the night pokemons when it's night time, since night only lasts for 1h and 45min.

Pokeballs painted blue means that the pokemon needs to be asleep. All catch are considered as 1hp.

Underscored pokemons means that it requires a lure to find it.

The "%" on some pokemons means that the chance to catch is not 100%.
